How CodeVault teaches

  1. 1. Concept. A 5-minute intro to the pattern with one diagram.
  2. 2. Anchor problem. Solve one classic problem from scratch.
  3. 3. Pattern variations. Solve three more — recognising the pattern is the goal.
  4. 4. Mastery. Show three different approaches (brute, ideal, idiomatic) → that becomes your shiver detector.
